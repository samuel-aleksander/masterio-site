masterio

masterio is a personal assistant that one person runs on their own Mac. It is not a service and has no other users: there is nothing to sign up for, and no server that holds anyone's data.

It reads its owner's Gmail with read-only access to summarize email, keep notes and draft replies for the owner to send themselves. It cannot send, delete or change email. It can also create Google Docs and Sheets of its own, and update one spreadsheet its owner chooses.
